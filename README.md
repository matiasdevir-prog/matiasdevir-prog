## 🔪 Verdugo

**[Verdugo](https://github.com/matiasdevir-prog/verdugo)** — the honest backtest
gauntlet that kills fake trading edges. It stress-tests any strategy **net of real
cost, out-of-sample, and across every year**, then gives one verdict: **PASS** or
**smoke**. Open-source, MIT.

## Verdugo isn't just theory — two of my strategies passed it

To prove the gauntlet cuts both ways (it doesn't only kill smoke — it also lets a
real edge through), two of my own forex strategies survived every stage of
Verdugo: positive **net of real cost**, holding up **out-of-sample**, and
**positive every year**.

- **AGUA** — a rebound strategy: it buys when price overstretches to the downside
  and springs back. It wins about **64%** of the time, but each win is worth
  **~2× the risk** (2:1). So out of 10 trades: ~6 wins worth 12, ~4 losses worth
  4 → clearly net positive. A 64% that most people would wrongly dismiss.
- **TIERRA** — a "spring" strategy: it sells when price overstretches to the
  upside. It wins the large majority of the time at roughly 1:1.

*(The **how** — the exact rules, instruments, and timing — stays private. What's
open here is the honest measuring tool, not the edge. And live trading is always
the final judge.)*
